How long does a wash take?

A Pit Stop takes about 20 minutes, a Cruiser about 40 minutes and a Mother Road about 1 hr 15 min. Each extra adds between 5 and 25 minutes.

What is included in each wash?

The Pit Stop is an exterior wash and dry. The Cruiser adds a vacuum, a dash wipe and the inside of the windows. The Mother Road adds a hand wax on top of that. The route on the home page shows every stage.

Do bigger vehicles cost more?

Yes. Each wash has a price for a hatch or sedan, an SUV or bakkie, and a minibus or van. The washes page shows the price for yours.
